Second base · Projects

Built on my own time

AI TPI Golf Coach

A computer-vision swing screen, end to end in Python

Upload a swing video and get a Titleist Performance Institute-style assessment. A custom-trained YOLOv8 detector and MediaPipe pose estimation track the body and club, then the app profiles the kinematic sequence (pelvis, torso, lead arm, trail arm), computes X-Factor hip-to-shoulder separation, and turns the numbers into coaching notes with the OpenAI API. Deployed with Shiny for Python.

Python · YOLOv8 · MediaPipe · OpenCV · Plotly · OpenAI · Shiny

FlickFlow

Swipe-based movie recommender, Georgia Tech CSE 6242, team of three

New users start on content-based filtering over roughly 100,000 TMDB titles. After 25 swipes the app hands off to a custom counts-and-means collaborative filter built on a cosine similarity matrix of users, with every interaction stored in MongoDB. In evaluation, 39 users made 579 swipes and the collaborative model beat the content-based cold start.

Python · MongoDB · TMDB API · Collaborative filtering
