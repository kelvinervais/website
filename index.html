Projects

AI TPI Golf Coach

A computer-vision swing screen, built end to end in Python.

Upload a swing video and get a Titleist Performance Institute-style assessment. A custom-trained YOLOv8 detector and MediaPipe pose estimation track the body and club through the swing. The app then profiles the kinematic sequence (pelvis, torso, lead arm, trail arm velocities), computes X-Factor hip-to-shoulder separation, and turns the measurements into plain-language coaching notes with the OpenAI API. Deployed with Shiny for Python.

FlickFlow

A swipe-based movie recommender with no streaming-service bias. Georgia Tech CSE 6242, team of three.

New users answer three questions and start on content-based filtering over roughly 100,000 TMDB titles. After 25 swipes the app hands off to a custom counts-and-means collaborative filter built on a cosine similarity matrix of users, with every interaction stored in MongoDB. In the evaluation period 39 users made 579 swipes, and the collaborative model beat the content-based cold start on right-swipe rate.
